About Lesotho

Discover the people, history and natural beauty of the Kingdom in the Sky.

Our Beautiful Country

Lesotho is a mountainous country in Southern Africa, surrounded by South Africa. It is known for its highlands, traditional Basotho culture and welcoming communities.

Visitors can enjoy scenic drives, hiking, pony trekking, local crafts and the country's distinctive mountain landscapes.

Why visit Lesotho?

  • Beautiful mountains and valleys.
  • Basotho culture and traditional clothing.
  • Outdoor adventures and nature.
  • Local food, crafts and hospitality.

Frequently Asked Questions

Lesotho is famous for its high mountains, Basotho culture, traditional blankets, pony trekking and beautiful scenery.

Visitors can enjoy hiking, pony trekking, sightseeing, photography and cultural experiences.

Visitors should prepare suitable clothing, comfortable shoes, water and weather-appropriate supplies for their planned activities.
